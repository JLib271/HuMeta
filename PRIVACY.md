# HuMeta Portal privacy policy

*Effective November 13, 2026*

The HuMeta Portal (https://humeta.streamlit.app/) helps researchers describe,
plan, and share studies. It is an open-source project maintained by Julie C.
Libarkin; its code is public at https://github.com/JLib271/HuMeta. This policy
explains what information the portal handles and what happens to it.

**In short:** you can use the portal without signing in, and we do not keep
the files you work with. If you sign in, we use your name to credit what you
choose to submit. Anything you submit becomes public on GitHub.

## Using the portal without signing in

You can build a study profile, plan measures, check metadata, and run
analyses without an account.

* **Files you open or upload** (project files, data files) are processed only
  while your session is open, to produce what you see and download. The portal
  does not save them, and they are gone when your session ends.
* **Your work is kept by you**, in the project file you download. The portal
  has no accounts, database, or saved projects.
* **We do not use advertising, analytics, or tracking tools**, and we do not
  sell or share information about you.

Please do not upload data that identifies research participants. Use
de-identified data, as your consent forms and ethics approvals require.

## Signing in with Google

Signing in is needed only to submit something for public listing (see below).
When you sign in, Google tells the portal your **name**, **email address**, and
an **account identifier**. The portal uses them while you are signed in to:

* show who is signed in, and
* credit your submissions with your **name**, plus a short code made by
  scrambling your account identifier (so maintainers can tell submissions from
  the same account apart). Your **email address is not published**.

The portal does not store this information after your session. A cookie in
your browser keeps you signed in until you sign out or it expires.

## What you submit is public

When you choose to submit something, it is sent to a public GitHub
repository for review:

| You submit | It goes to |
|---|---|
| Recommendations of theories, instruments, or corrections | [humeta-library](https://github.com/JLib271/humeta-library) (pull request) |
| Proposed changes to the HuMeta metadata profile | [humeta-profile](https://github.com/JLib271/humeta-profile) (issue) |
| Analysis recommendations, study summaries, datasets | [HuMeta](https://github.com/JLib271/HuMeta) (pull request) |
| Feedback from the "Send feedback" box | [HuMeta](https://github.com/JLib271/HuMeta) (issue) |

A submission, with your name if you were signed in, is **public as soon as it
is submitted** and stays in the repository's history. Maintainers can remove
it from current view on request, but copies made by others, and the history,
may remain. Do not include confidential study details or participant
information.

## Services we rely on

The portal runs on services with their own privacy policies, which apply to
the information they handle:

* **Streamlit Community Cloud** (Snowflake Inc.) hosts the portal and may log
  technical information such as IP addresses and usage, as any website host
  does. See [Streamlit's privacy page](https://streamlit.io/privacy), which points to the Snowflake Privacy Notice.
* **Google** handles sign-in. See the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
* **GitHub** hosts submissions, the code, and the HuMeta Library website the
  portal reads. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Your choices

* Use the portal without signing in.
* Sign out at any time from the sidebar.
* To ask for a submission to be removed or corrected, or for any privacy
  question, contact us as below.

The portal is meant for researchers and is not directed to children.

## Contact

Use GitHub's private reporting: go to the HuMeta repository's
[Security tab](https://github.com/JLib271/HuMeta/security) and choose
**Report a vulnerability**. Please do not post sensitive details in a public
issue.

## Changes

We will post any changes to this policy here, with a new effective date.
Earlier versions are in the [repository's history](https://github.com/JLib271/HuMeta/commits/main/PRIVACY.md).
