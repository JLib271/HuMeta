# Endorsing the SHE Study Metadata Profile

Version 1.0.0 of the profile will be submitted to scientific and professional
societies for formal endorsement. Endorsement turns a project specification
into a community standard: it signals that a field's own organizations
recommend it for describing research data, instruments, and studies in their
field. A society endorses the core and the field module (Education, Social
Science, or Humanities) that serves its members. Endorsements are listed in the profile and in its citation record.

## What endorsement means

An endorsing organization states that it:

1. Recommends the SHE Study Metadata Profile (version 1.0) and its [module]
   module to its members for describing research data, instruments, and
   studies when depositing or sharing them; and
2. May name a liaison to the profile's governance, to bring members' needs to
   future versions.

Endorsement does **not** commit the organization to funding, software, data
hosting, or any legal or compliance responsibility, and it does not endorse
any particular repository or product.

## Path to 1.0.0 (target: before the March 2, 2027 NSF PESOSE deadline)

| When | Step |
|---|---|
| Nov 2026 | Announce draft 1.0.0-draft.1; open public comment (GitHub issues labeled `proposal`, plus an email option for non-GitHub users) |
| Nov–Dec 2026 | Targeted review: researchers in education, the social sciences, and the humanities (one working group per module), research data librarians, IRB/research-compliance staff, and Indigenous data governance experts (for the CARE elements) |
| By mid-Dec 2026 | Close comment (at least 30 days); publish a response to every comment |
| Jan 2027 | Release 1.0.0; register its DOI (DataCite resourceTypeGeneral `Standard`) |
| Jan–Feb 2027 | Send endorsement requests; follow each society's process |
| Ongoing | Record endorsements below and in the citation metadata |

Society boards often meet quarterly, so a formal vote may not happen before
March. If so, ask for a **letter of support from the relevant committee or
officer** in the meantime. Letters from organizations and researchers outside
the project team who use or contribute to the profile can also serve as the
3–5 letters of collaboration the PESOSE solicitation requires.

## Candidate organizations

Choose two or three whose members are the profile's main users, and confirm
each organization's own process for endorsing external standards (usually a
board or policy-committee decision) before writing.

| Organization | Why it fits |
|---|---|
| National Association of Geoscience Teachers (NAGT) | Geoscience education research community; direct link to the GCI and climate instruments in the library |
| NARST (a global organization for improving science education through research) | Broad science education research community across disciplines |
| Society for the Advancement of Biology Education Research (SABER) | Large discipline-based education research community with strong measurement focus |
| American Association of Physics Teachers (AAPT) | Physics teaching and physics education research community, where many concept inventories originated |
| American Chemical Society, Division of Chemical Education | Chemistry education research community |
| ASEE Educational Research and Methods Division | Engineering education research |
| Association of Science and Technology Centers (ASTC) | Informal and public science learning (museums, science centers) |

Starting with the communities closest to the existing library (geoscience,
then one broad science-education society and one discipline-based society) is
likely to move fastest.
