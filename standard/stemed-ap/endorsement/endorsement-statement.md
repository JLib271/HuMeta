# Endorsement statement (for adoption by a society)

*A society may adopt this text as written or adapt it.*

---

**[Society] endorses the STEMEd+ Metadata Application Profile, version 1.0.**

[Society] recognizes the STEMEd+ Metadata Application Profile (version 1.0,
[DOI]) as a community standard for describing research data, instruments, and
studies in STEM education research. The profile records the constructs
measured, the population and context, the instructional setting, the type of
evidence, consent and access conditions, and Indigenous data governance
following the CARE Principles, and it maps to DataCite and the ERIC Thesaurus.

[Society] recommends that its members use the profile when depositing,
registering, or sharing research data, instruments, and study materials, so
that work in our field is findable, comparable, and reused responsibly.

obligations for [Society] and does not endorse any particular repository,
product, or service.
