**Subject:** Request for [Society] endorsement of the STEMEd+ Metadata Application Profile 1.0

Dear [Name],

I am writing to ask whether [Society] would consider endorsing version 1.0 of
the **STEMEd+ Metadata Application Profile**, an open metadata standard for
describing STEM education research data, instruments, and studies.

Researchers in our field increasingly share data and instruments, but general
repositories cannot record what makes them reusable: which constructs were
measured and with what instrument, who participated and in what instructional
setting, what kind of evidence the study produced, and what participants
consented to regarding sharing. The profile adds these elements, along with
fields for Indigenous data governance based on the CARE Principles, and maps
everything to DataCite (used for DOIs) and the ERIC Thesaurus so it works
with the tools people already use. It is free and openly licensed (CC BY-SA
4.0), and it is implemented in OpenEdMetadata, an open-source app that helps
researchers fill it in.

Version 1.0 was finalized after a public comment period that included
[number] reviewers from [disciplines/roles]. [One sentence on a change made
in response to comments, if helpful.]

Endorsement would mean that [Society] recommends the profile to its members
for describing research data, instruments, and studies, and, if you wish,
names a liaison to help guide future versions. It carries no financial,
technical, or compliance obligations. The full endorsement statement is
attached.
